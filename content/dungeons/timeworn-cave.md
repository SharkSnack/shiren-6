Mystery Dungeon that aims to be more like a classic final dungeon from previous games in the series.<br/>Shiren 6 mechanics like behemoths, cannons, bouncy walls, sacred gear, and wishing shrines don't appear.<br/>That might sound similar to Researcher's Experimental Site, but Mixers can be found in this dungeon.<br/>It features a unique monster and item table, instead of being a derivative of Heart of Serpentcoil Island or such.<br/>There's a significant difference in quality between items found on the ground and items found in other ways.<br/>Overall, it's basically a dungeon for players who want a more straightforward and traditional challenge.

# Strategy

### Dungeon Characteristics

#### Unique Monster Table

The monster table features lots of monsters that don't naturally appear in Heart of Serpentcoil Island.<br/>Monster strength ramps up somewhat faster than most other dungeons (ex: 11F Pumpanshee, 15F Big Chintala).<br/>Field Knaves and Gazers appear in the 10F range, Brodillo, Nigiri Morph, Curse Girl, Pot Knave in the 20F range,<br/>Fencer from 37F - In other words, monsters that target items appear consistently throughout the dungeon.<br/>Lv3-4 Dragons and Miracle Hen don't appear, but instead Porkon, Skull Lord, and Sleep Radish show up.<br/>In particular, Lv3-4 Swordsman, Porkon, and Skull Lord appear on a wide range of floors near the end.<br/>1 type of incense isn't enough to counter all threats, so Eradication Scroll, runes, and bracelets are essential.<br/>Nigiri Baby doesn't spawn, but Nigiri Morph appears on 27F, so Nigiri Morph Factory can still be performed.

#### Unique Item Table

The item table seems to have a big difference in quality between floor items and item found in other ways,<br/>with the latter methods including things like shiny wall tiles, items islands, Chow drops, and dungeon shops.<br/>Island items can be claimed using Earthmound Staff, Walrus Pot, or by utilizing an Aquatic or Floating type foe.<br/>Avoid using non-floor table unidentified items right away, since Eradication Scroll or Ill-fated Seed are possible.<br/>It's best to store them separately from other items, and wait until you find an Identifier Scroll or shop.

#### High-Value Items in Shops

Floor table items are usually lower quality, so it's important to locate shops and obtain merchandise.<br/>Shops aren't very uncommon, but they can be generated buried in wall tiles like Heart of Serpentcoil Island.<br/>Some players swear that merchandise is higher quality in buried shops, but it's hard to give a definitive answer.<br/>Nonetheless, these shops can have items like Upgrading Pot, Blank or Eradication scrolls, or rare gear for sale.<br/>Blank Scrolls are much harder to come by than Heart of Serpentcoil Island, so think carefully before using them.<br/>Nigiri Baby doesn't spawn → Sumo status isn't available, so it's difficult to enter buried shops early game.<br/>Monster-find or Item-find bracelets let you spot shops, and Leaping Bracelet or Burrowing Staff grant entry.<br/>Lastly, it's said that 10F, 25F, 51F, and 75F are nearly guaranteed to generate a dungeon shop.

### General Advice

#### Early Game Tips

Pumpanshee (11-14F) and Big Chintala (15-21F) are the early game power monsters in this dungeon.<br/>Don't hesitate to use staves and other resources in an effort to survive until the Mixer floor range.

Nigiri Morph (27-31F) provides a chance for you to raise Max Fullness to 200 and activate Sumo status.<br/>Sumo status acts as a method of entry for buried shops, so don't be lazy and skip Nigiri Morph Factory.<br/>Prepare items like Unlucky Staff and a shield that doesn't have any payback runes in advance.

#### Upgrading Equipment

Sacred items can't be found, so rune slots are going to be limited (Slot-adding Scroll appears in shops).<br/>While it's tempting to choose gear with lots of rune slots, base value is the priority for early\~mid game.<br/>Aim for weapon and shield strengths of around 11+ by the time Big Chintala (15-21F) shows up.<br/>Doutanuki and Wolfshead resonate for +3 item strength, making it an effective main set early on.<br/>Maining gear with few rune slots has the advantage of being able to synthesize for upgrade value easily,<br/>since once rune slots are full, you can fuse Break-Off Blade+1 or such without transferring bad runes.

It's extremely important to hold on to gear with positive upgrade value to synthesize later on.<br/>Thankfully Mixers, Synthesis Pots, and pots to store synthesis ingredients in are all not super rare.<br/>Safely check upgrade value for equipment by comparing prices in a shop, or by using a Removal Trap.<br/>Fixer, Plating, Heavenly, Earthly, and Exorcism scrolls lift curses and let you check without a shop or trap,<br/>so some players like to prioritize inserting unidentified scrolls into Identifier Pots early on.

Mixer (16-36F), Mixermon (45-57F), Mixergon (58-83F), and Mixerdon (84-99F) are the Mixer family floors.<br/>In other words, the only floor ranges without the possibility of Mixers spawning is 1-15F and 37-44F.<br/>You'll ideally want roughly 15 item strength by 20F, and 20 item strength + some decent runes by 29F.<br/>It's worth carrying even gear with only +1 upgrade value considering the wide floor range Mixers spawn on,<br/>though of course you'll want to keep your inventory balanced enough to deal with tricky situations if needed.

#### Utilizing Shops

Shops often have higher quality items, so aim to obtain merchandise one way or another.<br/>Blank Scroll, Upgrading Pot, Kama Itachi, Axe of the Minotaur, Clairvoyant Bracelet, and Appraiser's Bracelet<br/>are just some of the notable items that can be found in shops, so it's important to know how to steal.<br/>Items like Mapping Scroll, Monster-find Bracelet, and Item-find Bracelet can be used to spot buried shops.<br/>If you lack the above but have a way to dig through walls, use the <span class="blueText">Look Around</span> command to search room edges.<br/>Shops are common, so remember to sell items before stealing to make it easier to buy items in later shops.

#### Inventory Tips

Scrolls, staves, and other consumables that help you deal with stronger threats are common.<br/>Preservation Pots are also pretty common, so keep a balance of synthesis ingredients and consumables.<br/>If you don't need to use an item right away, insert it into a pot like Ordinary or Handtrapper pot.<br/>There are usually lots of synthesis chances, so remember to combine staves to free up inventory space.<br/>However, don't neglect to swing new staves 1 time to check for curses before synthesizing them.

### Important Items

#### Main Weapon

Expect to burn resources and ultimately lose the run if you attempt to main a low attack power weapon early on.<br/>Sets with high attack power (Katana, Doutanuki, Manji Kabura) should be used until you reach Mixers on 16F.<br/>After that, aim to transfer to a high rune slot weapon like Golden Sword, Sickle of Salvation, or Primal Axe.<br/>However, all-out power builds like Manji Kabura (Frontal Attack + Costly Hit + Voracious Hit) are also viable.

#### Weapon Runes

Runes that are effective against all monster families are a higher priority than type-effective runes.<br/>Peach Club and Healing Sword have been sighted, but they're so rare that it's unrealistic to include their runes.<br/>Costly Hit and Voracious Hit are powerful, but don't synthesize them until you have plenty of Gitan / Fullness.

#### Main Shield

Sets with high defense (Iron Shield, Wolfshead, Fuuma Shield) should be used until you reach Mixers on 16F,<br/>then choose a main shield with 5 or more rune slots, and use Slot-adding Scrolls if you need more slots.<br/>Golden Shield (Rustproof + 9 slots) and Wooden Shield (7 slots) are likely the highest slot shields,<br/>followed by Bronze Shield (6 slots), Counter Shield (Retribution + 5 slots), and Watchful Shield (Keen + 5 slots).<br/>Auspicious Omamori (Tough At 7 + 6 slots) and Kappa's Dish (Projectileproof + 6 slots) are shop-exclusive.

#### Shield Runes

The best runes for this dungeon are Jitter Payback, Onigiri Payback, Keen, Retribution, and Sating.<br/>Onigiri Scroll is on the rare side and troublesome to identify, so consider writing it on a Blank Scroll.<br/>The Rustproof rune is unnecessary for Mudsters (54-62F) if you manage to find a Rustproof Bracelet.<br/>Heat-ban Incense or Eradication Scroll (Blank Scroll) is an effective substitute for the Anti-Blast rune,<br/>but Reflective Incense alone isn't enough to counter the entire floor range for Skull Lords (77-99F).

#### Bracelets

Monster-find, Clairvoyant, Tiptoe, Appraiser's, Wallpass, and Haggling bracelets have been seen in shops.<br/>Meanwhile Far-throwing, Strength, Fortune, Dreaded, Trapmore, Bad-aim, Rousing, Cursebreak, Cleansing,<br/>Focusing, Leaping, Waterwalk, and Passage-find bracelets have been found on the ground.<br/>1500 Gitan bracelets are very common, but most are Leaping, Far-throwing, Trapmore, Blasting, and Dreaded.<br/>Item-losing and Gitan-losing bracelets are currently thought to be excluded from the item table.

<p><b><u>Leaping Bracelet</u></b><br/>Can be found on the ground, and lets you enter buried shops and Gitan rooms for free.<br/>Avoid surprise attacks from Curse Girl, Nigiri Morph, etc. in hallways by warping to other rooms.<br/>Activate the effect by walking to ensure safety, instead of by stepping in place or swinging your weapon.</p>

<p><b><u>Far-throwing Bracelet</u></b><br/>Can be found on the ground, and it's actually pretty common in this dungeon.<br/>Shines when you have lots of arrows, or when you have high-value Gitan bags in your inventory.</p>

<p><b><u>Strength Bracelet</u></b><br/>Strength Grass and Special Onigiri are rare, and Kron's Challenges and Wishing Shrines are never generated,<br/>so it's surprisingly difficult to increase Max Strength in Timeworn Cave compared to other dungeons.<br/>Therefore, this bracelet can be helpful for boosting damage output, especially early~mid game.</p>

<p><b><u>Waterwalk Bracelet</u></b><br/>Enter a waterway to safely regenerate HP on floors where Aquatic and Floating types don't appear.<br/>Unequip the bracelet while standing on a water tile to warp elsewhere, even during Sumo status.<br/>Also lets you take shortcuts, but beware of scrolls and food items that aren't in pots getting wet.</p>

<p><b><u>Rousing Bracelet</u></b><br/>Protects against Asleep status caused by Sleep Trap, Sleep Radish (84-99F), and Skull Lord (77-99F).<br/>Battler (77-83F) or Swordmaster (91-99F) can potentially disarm you while you're asleep if you lack this.<br/>Though that said, Sleep Radishes usually aren't super common on the floors where they spawn.</p>

<p><b><u>Affixing Bracelet</u></b><br/>Protects your equipped items from being disarmed by Swordsman family monsters.<br/>Swordsman family floors include Fencer (37-44F), Battler (77-83F), and Swordmaster (91-99F).</p>

<p><b><u>Cursebreak Bracelet</u></b><br/>Curse Girl (20-28F) overlaps with Mixer (16-36F), so this helps prevent being unable to synthesize your shield.<br/>Curse Girl family floors include Curse Girl (20-28F) and Curse Matron (63-66F).</p>

<p><b><u>Fortune Bracelet</u></b><br/>Nice bracelet to find near the start of a run, and can be used to counteract level-down effects.<br/>Consider equipping it on floors where Twisty Hani family monsters, Skullmancer, and Skull Lord appear.<br/>Of course, prioritize Rousing Bracelet against Skull Lord if you actually have that bracelet.</p>

#### Pots / Incenses

Preservation and Synthesis pots are somewhat common.<br/>The available incense types seem to be limited to Evasive, Reflective, Heat-ban, and Cautious.<br/>Upgrading Pot and Degrading Pot have been reportedly seen on the ground, but they're very rare.

<p><b><u>Handtrapper Pot</u></b><br/>Insert an item, then use the stairs or step on a Removal Trap or Onigiri Trap to instantly free your hand.<br/>Invincible Grass can also be eaten beforehand to insert items without your hand getting stuck in the pot.<br/>It can also be used to unequip a cursed weapon, essentially letting you identify weapons in a pinch.</p>

<p><b><u>Upgrading Pot</u></b><br/>Can be found on the ground, in a shop, or from a wall tile.<br/>Think carefully about what item category you want to upgrade - Remember, staves are an option too.</p>

<p><b><u>Rejuvenation Pot</u></b><br/>The ultimate HP, strength, and status recovery item.<br/>If you're low on food, consider tapping this pot instead of stepping in place to recover HP.</p>

<p><b><u>Bottomless Pot</u></b><br/>Steal from a shop risk-free, but remember to break it outside of the carpeted area.<br/>Can be used during Sumo status if you break it while standing next to a wall → throw an item at the wall.</p>

<p><b><u>Heat-ban Incense</u></b><br/>Incense that basically exists to counter Pop Tank family monsters.<br/>Pop Tank family floors include Ornery Tank (45-50F), Adamant Tank (45-50F), and Steadfast Tank (51-56F).<br/>The tank floor range is narrower, so use this incense instead of a Blank Scroll to get past it if possible.</p>

<p><b><u>Reflective Incense</u></b><br/>Protects against magic bullets and many Draining type monster special attacks.<br/>However, Skull Mage family monsters appear on a wide floor range, so this isn't enough to counter them.</p>

<p><b><u>Cautious Incense</u></b><br/>Protects against monster special attacks that either steal or disarm your items.<br/>Swordsman family monsters appear on a wide floor range, so this isn't enough to counter them.</p>

<p><b><u>Evasive Incense</u></b><br/>Collect Porky's Rocks from Porky (6-10F), and Wooden Arrows from Bowboy (11-12F).<br/>Keeps you safe from Porkon (77-99F) and Sleep Radish (84-99F)'s projectiles later on.</p>

#### Scrolls

Confusion, Slumber, and Jitters scrolls are somewhat common.<br/>Blank and Eradication scrolls appear in shops, but neither scroll is exactly on the common side.<br/>Silverpurge, Silver-seal, Wall-less, and Expulsion scrolls are currently thought to be excluded from the item table.

<p><b><u>Blank Scroll</u></b><br/>Can be obtained from shops, wall tiles, or Transmutation Pots.<br/>It's best to save 1 to use as an Eradication Scroll against either Skull Lord or Swordsman family monsters.<br/>In any case, use Blank Scrolls very carefully, since you likely won't find many of them in any given run.</p>

<p><b><u>Eradication Scroll</u></b><br/>Obtainable from shops and wall tiles, meaning you might read an unidentified one by accident.<br/>Targets: Skull Mage family, Swordsman family, Sleep Radish, King Reaper, Mudster, Terror Scorpion, Pop Tank family.<br/>If you have a Rousing Bracelet, consider eradicating the Swordsman family or Mudster over Skull Lord.</p>

<p><b><u>Jitters Scroll, Onigiri Scroll</u></b><br/>Both are Cross-Mixing synthesis ingredients for fantastic "Payback" shield runes.<br/>Jitters Scroll isn't too rare, but Onigiri Scroll is quite rare.</p>

<p><b><u>Slot-adding Scroll</u></b><br/>Obtainable from shops, wall tiles, and item islands.<br/>This dungeon never generates sacred items, so additional rune slots are quite valuable.<br/>Side note, but Rune-eraser Scroll hasn't been sighted in this dungeon yet.</p>

<p><b><u>Monstercall Scroll</u></b><br/>Dangerous when read without adequate preparation, but provides a chance to obtain more items.<br/>Never read it on floors where item-destroying monsters like Field Knave or Fire Ninja D spawn.</p>

<p><b><u>Gambler's Scroll</u></b><br/>10,000 Gitan can be a nice bonus if you get lucky with this scroll at the start of a run,<br/>but reading this scroll can effectively end your run later on if you've synthesized Costly Hit or Costly Block.<br/>Therefore, stop reading unidentified scrolls from mid game unless you've already identified this scroll.</p>

#### Staves

Staves are common in this dungeon, so it's important to combine same name staves using Mixers.<br/>Check if a newly found staff is cursed by swinging it 1 time, especially before throwing the staff at a Mixer.<br/>Guiding Staff has been reported, but it seems to be incredibly rare - Peach Staff likely can't be found.

<p><b><u>Skull Mage's Staff</u></b><br/>Can be found on the ground or in shops - The random effect of the magic bullet isn't exactly reliable,<br/>but making it a point to use this staff often results in conserving the charges of better staves.<br/>It's tricky to discern between Skull Mage's Staff and Sluggish Staff if the Slow effect occurs.</p>

<p><b><u>Paralyzing Staff</u></b><br/>Powerful staff that completely shuts down a monster, excluding the Hoppin' Batter family.<br/>Also serves as a Cross-Mixing synthesis ingredient for the Paralyzing weapon rune.</p>

<p><b><u>Knockback Staff</u></b><br/>Swing this staff → shoot arrows to safely defeat Mixers or other monsters that lack ranged attacks.<br/>Can also be used to one-shot Ghost Samurai and Cave Mamel.</p>

<p><b><u>Burrowing Staff</u></b><br/>Key item that grants entry into buried shops early on before you activate Sumo status.<br/>Feel free to discard it once you've reached a point where you're sustaining Sumo status.</p>

<p><b><u>Narrow-escape Staff</u></b><br/>Use it with a Monster-find Bracelet to locate the stairs, or Reflective Incense to warp to the stairs.<br/>Some players like to prioritize inserting this staff into Upgrading Pot.</p>

<p><b><u>Fortune Staff</u></b><br/>Level up a Mixer to synthesize more items, obtain Huge or Special onigiri from higher level Egg Things,<br/>or hunt Cave Mamels (or Gitan Mamels if you also want Gitan) to level up near the start of a run.</p>

#### Grass

<p><b><u>Strength Grass</u></b><br/>Quite rare in this dungeon, so finding 2 or more Strength Grass isn't a given.<br/>If you want to equip 2 bracelets at the same time, plan on farming Special Onigiri from Hiding Eggs.</p>

<p><b><u>Revival Grass</u></b><br/>The monster table ramps up in power quickly, so it's ideal to identify this grass early on.</p>

<p><b><u>Swift Grass, Invincible Grass</u></b><br/>Powerful grasses that let you deal with crowds or tricky situations if needed.<br/>These grasses aren't very common, but they're not particularly rare either.</p>

<p><b><u>Ill-fated Seed</u></b><br/>Players have reportedly obtained it from shiny wall tiles, Chow, and item islands so far.<br/>It probably isn't in the actual floor table, so grasses found on the ground (non-island) should be safe.</p>

#### Food

Food is rarer than in Heart of Serpentcoil Island - It's common to go multiple floors without finding food.<br/>Nigiri Baby doesn't appear, and you step in place to recover HP a lot thanks to hard-hitting monsters,<br/>so it's surprisingly easy to collapse from starvation during early game prior to Nigiri Morph (27-31F) floors.<br/>If you have 2 or fewer food items on hand, it's probably best to hurry to the next floor instead of lingering.<br/>Linger for Mixers in moderation prior to performing Nigiri Morph Factory on 27F.

Egg Thing (19-20F), Flying Egg (40-44F), and Hiding Egg (72-80F) can be hunted for onigiri drops.<br/>You can't get duplicate effects aside from Strength +1 when eating Special Onigiri on the same floor,<br/>so eat all of them on the same floor if you want the best odds for Strength +1 or Knowledgeable status.<br/>Sumo Onigiri hasn't been sighted so far, and is currently thought to be excluded from the item table.<br/>Sweet Potato is another item that appears to be excluded from the item table.

#### Projectiles

Arrows can be collected from Bowboy (11-12F), or from various arrow traps on other floors.<br/>Behemoths don't appear, but rocks are still nice for Masked Samurai family and Cave Mamel floors.

### Farming Techniques

#### Cave Mamel / Gitan Mamel Hunting (1-3F)

It's possible to level up Pit Mamel (1-3F) using Disguising or Fortune staves, Jitters Scroll, etc.<br/>Player level is extremely important for surviving early game power monsters before you reach Mixers on 16F.<br/>Cave Mamel is best if you just want to level up, but Gitan Mamel provides 2,000 Gitan each for shops.

#### Porky's Rock Collecting (6-10F)

Evasive Incense can be used to collect Porky's Rocks from Porky (6-10F).

#### Arrow Collecting (11-12F)

Soldier Ant (7-11F) and Bowboy (11-12F) overlap on 11F, so you can use terrain to collect arrows.<br/>Otherwise, you'll probably need an item like Earthmound Staff or Evasive Incense to collect arrows.<br/>If you want Iron Arrows instead of Wooden Arrows, simply have the Bowboy defeat a monster beforehand.

#### Nigiri Morph Factory (27-31F)

Nigiri Morph (27-31F)'s special attack has a 100% chance to turn an inventory item into a Large Onigiri.<br/>However, it has higher stats (85 HP, 35 atk, 30 def), so you'll want to lower its level or attack power first.<br/>Poison Arrows, Poison Grass, Sluggish Staff, and Bad-aim Bracelet + Removal or Hunger traps are helpful.<br/>Use a secondary shield if your main shield has Jitter Payback, Onigiri Payback, or Retribution runes.<br/>Lastly, beware of Porko pelting you with Porky's Rocks, and read a Mapping Scroll if possible.

#### Greenwalrus Hunting (28-32F)

Greenwalrus (28-32F) can be hunted for guaranteed item drops if you can deal with other threats.<br/>The Frontal Attack rune, Monster-find and Cursebreak bracelets, and Mapping Scroll are all helpful.<br/>Keep in mind that you can use Blast Traps or Dragon's flame to grill onigiri if you have Onigiri Payback.

##### Notable Threats for Lingering

- 28F - Nigiri Morph, Curse Girl
- 29-31F - Nigiri Morph, Pot Knave, Daze Radish
- 32F - Daze Radish

### 15-22F

<p><span class="highlightYellow">Notable:</span> Mudkin (15-21F), <span class="orangeText">Big Chintala (15-21F)</span>, <span class="blueText">Mixer (16-36F)</span>, Okina Monk D (18-20F), <span class="lightBlueText">Egg Thing (19-20F)</span>,<br/><span class="lightBlueText">Thiefwalrus (19-22F)</span>, Tengu Monk D (20-21F), Hannya Monk D (20-23F), Brodillo (20-26F), Curse Girl (20-28F)</p>

Big Chintala (15-21F) has a whopping 85 HP and 40 attack power despite appearing as early as 15F.<br/>Always fight it 1 vs 1, and either retreat to heal HP, shoot a Poison Arrow, or swing a staff as needed.<br/>Mudkin (15-21F) lowers equipment upgrade value, which makes it even harder to survive encounters.<br/>The Rustproof rune helps, but remember that you have fewer rune slots to work with in this dungeon.<br/>As an alternative, keep 2 slots open in a Preservation Pot and insert (multi-select) your gear when adjacent.<br/>This technique is also helpful for when Okina Monk D (18-20F) warps Mudkins right next to you.

Lv1 Okina & Kitsune (18-20F), Tengu (20-21F), and Hannya (20-23F) monks appear between 18-23F.<br/>Thiefwalrus (19-22F) often gets woken up, and Big Chintala or Brodillo can level up due to Berserk status.<br/>Worst case scenario, a Swift speed Huge Chintala or such can potentially be warped next to you on 20F.<br/>Monks also easily cause accidents while trying to synthesize with Mixers, so just rush to the stairs.<br/>That said, if you have an Evasive Incense or an applicable bracelet, you can obtain Confusion or Sedating grass<br/>for weapon rune synthesis purposes from a leveled up Ghost Radish thanks to Hannya Monk D on 20F.<br/>Egg Thing (19-20F) always drops an Onigiri when slain, which can be grilled by Pop Tank (19-21F).

Brodillo (20-26F) has stats comparable to Big Chintala and can cause pots to break from tripping.<br/>Its ability usage rate is high, so Balance Staff runs out of uses quickly if you need more than 2 hits to slay it.<br/>Sumo status is hard to activate at this point, so shoot arrows → finish it off with a normal attack instead.<br/>Also beware of the combo of being tripped → Pop Tank destroying dropped items on 20-21F.

Curse Girl (20-28F) curses your equipped shield, making you unable to synthesize shield runes.<br/>Similar to Brodillo, shoot arrows from a distance → finish Curse Girl off with a normal attack.<br/>If she's too close, either swing a staff or unequip your shield if you're high level and have plenty of HP.<br/>The latter will be quite painful, but it's better than being unable to synthesize assuming you survive.

### 23-31F

<p><span class="highlightYellow">Notable:</span> <span class="blueText">Mixer (16-36F)</span>, Brodillo (20-26F), Curse Girl (20-28F), Water Ninja C (24-26F), Porko (27-30F),<br/>Nigiri Morph (27-31F), N'dulu (27-31F), <span class="lightBlueText">Greenwalrus (28-32F)</span>, Dark Owl (28-99F), Pot Knave (29-31F),<br/>Daze Radish (29-32F), Dragon (29-33F), <span class="orangeText">Great Hen (29-36F)</span>, Sky Dragon (30-36F), Cave Mamel (30-58F)</p>

<p><span class="highlightShop">A shop is guaranteed to be generated on 25F</span>, unless it gets overwritten by a maze or Monster House.<br/>Lv2 Ninjas include Tree & Earth (23-24F), and Metal & Water (24-26F) - Fire Ninja C doesn't appear at all.<br/>Lingering for Mixers between 24-26F lets you collect Gitan from Metal Ninja C for shops at the same time.</p>

Nigiri Morph (27-31F)'s special attack has a 100% chance to turn 1 inventory item into Large Onigiri.<br/>Nigiri Morph Factory can be performed if you need food or want to activate Sumo status, but it can be tricky<br/>since Soldier Ant family monsters (27-30F) prevent sealing off rooms, and Pot Knave (29-31F) also appears.<br/>You'll also need to withstand attacks from Daze Radish, Porko, and Curse Girl in addition to Nigiri Morph.<br/>Your best bet is to use Poison Arrows or an Unlucky Staff against Nigiri Morph, then light an Evasive Incense.<br/>If you have Bad-aim and Trapmore bracelets, skip Nigiri Morph Factory and use traps to obtain food instead.<br/>Overall, don't push yourself too hard to obtain food here unless you're using the Voracious Hit weapon rune.

Get in the habit of separating synthesis ingredients and important items into separate pots.<br/>This reduces the risk of losing everything at once if an entire pot gets turned into a Large Onigiri.<br/>N'dulu (27-31F) also spawns, so be cautious when picking up items you find on the ground.

Daze Radish (29-32F), Dragon (29-33F), Sky Dragon (30-36F), and Great Hen (29-36F) can be rough.<br/>Great Hen has 55 attack power, so think of it like a Swift 1 Sky Dragon without a special attack.<br/>Greenwalrus (28-32F) can be hunted for items, but consider that Pot Knave and Nigiri Morph are around.<br/>Losing pot capacity or other valuable items to these 2 monsters would defeat the point of lingering,<br/>so it's probably best to rush to the stairs until 32F unless you have a Monster-find Bracelet.

Dark Owl (28-99F) isn't too troublesome on its own, but be wary if other monsters are nearby.<br/>Don't underestimate its ability to cause accidents on floors where foes with nasty special attacks spawn.<br/>Last but not least, Cave Mamel (30-58F) can surprise you with 100 attack power in dark hallways.
