# Strategy

<h3 id="dungeon-characteristics" class="heading3Yellow">Dungeon Characteristics</h3>

Most items generated are Gitan bags aside from items in walls, and shops are extremely common.<br/>Nazoh (Peddler) also appears, so there will be plenty of chances to obtain Gitan and go shopping.<br/>It goes without saying, but most non-Gitan bag items will have to be purchased or stolen from shops,<br/>and the kind of dungeon shop or exact lineup of merchandise you'll come across is of course random.<br/>Specialty shops that only stock one item category can appear, like a shop that only sells scrolls.

<h4 id="extra-notes" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/weapon/unknown.png"/> Extra Notes</h4>

- Monsters are stronger than [Sumo Dungeon](/dungeons/sumo-dungeon), [Dune of Batsu](/dungeons/dune-of-batsu), and [Training Path of Gimmicks](/dungeons/training-path-of-gimmicks).
    - Minotaurs and other enemies from [Serpentcoil Island](/dungeons/serpentcoil-island)'s mid-game floors appear from 3F.
    - Starting from 10F, the monster table is updated with tricky monsters every 5\~10 floors.
    - 31-40F features very dangerous monsters like Miracle Hen, Nigiri Honcho, and Nimble Knave.
- Flowing water and rafts are somewhat common, and a Porkon or monk on a raft can be tricky.
- Monster Houses are uncommon, but Sudden and Special styles can be generated on occasion.
- Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated between 2-19F, 21-39F, and 41-98F.
- Overall, it's said to be the 3rd hardest dungeon among those that allow bringing items.
    - The harder dungeons are [Isle of the Mighty](/dungeons/isle-of-the-mighty) and [Clash of Weapons and Shields](/dungeons/clash-of-weapons-and-shields).
- It's important to synthesize defensive runes to block as many special attacks as possible.
    - Don't forget to bring a powerful weapon, shield, and a variety of bracelets as well.

<h4 id="item-table" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/gitan.png"/> Item Table</h4>

The floor table mostly consists of Gitan bags so a player is bound to assume that other items<br/>can only be obtained from shops, but <span class="orangeText">non-Gitan items are often generated in wall tiles here</span>.<br/>The frequency is also high, perhaps with around half or more of the floors featuring buried items.<br/>Therefore, it's best to bring items like Burrowing Staff, Item-find Bracelet, Collection Scroll, etc.<br/>Shiny wall tiles and single tile pillars in rooms can also be destroyed by any normal attack,<br/>and unlike digging with a Pickaxe, your equipped weapon's upgrade value won't decrease.

While slain monsters mostly drop Gitan bags if they drop an item, certain families drop other items.<br/>These families include the likes of Minotaur (3-6F), Ironwalrus (11-30F), Magicwalrus (31-40F), etc.<br/>Also, items generated by reading a Monstercall Scroll will also mostly be Gitan bags.

To be precise, weapon and shield category items can rarely be generated even in this dungeon.<br/>However, this is currently thought to be exclusive to 1F, and the chance is still extremely low.<br/>Only basic gear (Katana, Fuuma Shield, etc.) and the Break-Off set have been sighted so far.

<h4 id="dungeon-gimmick-&-trap" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/bouncy_wall.png"/> Dungeon Gimmicks & Traps</h4>

Bouncy Walls, Fever Times, Kron's Challenges, and Wishing Shrines can be encountered here.<br/>Unfortunately, <span class="orangeText">Item Fever</span> features floor table items, so it mostly generates Gitan bags.<br/>Tunnel of Wishes adjust the item table to match the wish, so enemies can drop other items.

A wider variety of traps appear compared to Serpentcoil Island, but unlike Dune of Batsu or such,<br/>Feeding and Starvation traps are never generated, so the player's Max Fullness is safe.<br/>It seems like Silver-seal, Curse, Gassy, Summoning, and Pitfall traps have a high rate.<br/>The first two traps in the above list are quite troublesome, so Sumo status is a significant help.<br/>The stairs are descending, so Pitfall Trap can be used to safely steal from a dungeon shop.<br/>Blast and Explosion traps can be used to make Grilled Onigiri, helping with HP management<br/>when relying on Wallpass Bracelet to travel through walls when stealing from a dungeon shop.<br/>Gassy Trap is handy when solo, but backfires by separating you from companions in a party.<br/>Poison Arrow Trap can appear, but it's rare so don't expect to collect lots of Poison Arrows.

<h3 id="restricted-play-(start-from-sozoro-bay)" class="heading3Red">Restricted Play (Start from Sozoro Bay)</h3>

Challenge where you enter Sozoro Bay without bringing items, go to Underwater Training Ground,<br/>then enter the first clear depth of Training Path of Shopping with the items you've collected.<br/>Expect a solid challenge thanks to weak gear options and powerful foes in the monster table,<br/>but an early game Wallpass Bracelet or Travel Companion can turn the tide even with a bad start.<br/>Generally speaking, it's hard to clear this challenge if you don't find a strong option by 11F.

<h3 id="restricted-play-(no-bringing-items)" class="heading3Red">Restricted Play (No Bringing Items)</h3>

On paper, it's possible to clear this dungeon's first clear depth without bringing any items.<br/>1-2F has high value Gitan bags on the ground, so with some shop luck, you can get a run going.<br/>That said, it basically requires either an early Wallpass Bracelet or Travel Companion,<br/>so expect to restart the dungeon a lot - especially due to Death Reapers appearing on 1F.<br/>Overall, this challenge is not recommended over the "Start from Sozoro Bay" version.

<h4 id="bracelets" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/bracelet.png"/> Bracelets</h4>

<p><b><u>Monster-find Bracelet, Item-find Bracelet, Clairvoyant Bracelet</u></b><br/>Monster-find Bracelet lets you detect incoming dangerous threats in dark hallways,<br/>and also makes it easier to spot shops and Nazoh or other Roamers present on the floor.<br/>Item-find Bracelet is mostly used to locate items that are buried in wall tiles, and to detect shops.<br/>Clairvoyant Bracelet is a combo of the above, but it likely isn't in this dungeon's item table.</p>

<p><b><u>Affixing Bracelet</u></b><br/>Protects your items from Bag Knave (11-30F), Nimble Knave (31-50F), and Swordmaster (60-70F).<br/>Pots in your inventory that get knocked away by Weeds almost always shatter and create a mess,<br/>and having this bracelet also lets you throw Eradication Scroll at a different threat instead.</p>

<p><b><u>Trapper's Bracelet</u></b><br/>This dungeon has descending stairs, so you can steal using a Pitfall Trap.<br/>The Pitfall Trap must be placed outside of the shop's carpeted area, or else the trap will vanish.<br/>Can be used during Sumo status if placed next to a wall → shoot an arrow at the wall to trigger it.<br/>Pitfall Trap cannot be used to clear the dungeon if the current floor is the final floor of the dungeon.<br/>Trapper's Bracelet is primarily obtained by playing through <a href="/shiren-6/dungeons/trapper's-secret-path">Trapper's Secret Path</a>.</p>

<p><b><u>Wallpass Bracelet</u></b><br/>Among the best finds within this dungeon's shop table lineup, and thankfully not super uncommon.<br/>Lets you pass through walls, but you take damage equal to <span class="oceanBlueText">4%</span> of your Max HP per turn inside a wall.<br/>Some players keep this + Monster-find Bracelet on to avoid all adjacent special attackers,<br/>instead of preparing separate bracelets or items to counter Curse Matron, Swordmaster, etc.<br/>Wallpass Bracelet's price is <span class="oceanBlueText">6,500</span> Gitan and can be identified by walking next to the shop's wall,<br/>or if the shop has unbreakable walls, try attacking a foe through a corner near the shop's entrance.</p>

<h4 id="other-items" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/grass.png"/> <img src="../images/other/scroll.png"/> <img src="../images/other/rock.png"/> Other Items</h4>

<p><b><u>Peach Club (<span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/weapon/27.png"/></span> Peach Bun), Peach Staff</u></b><br/>Turn most any monster into a Peach Bun and and eat it replenish Fullness as needed.<br/>Mixergon (16-30F) can be turned into a Mixergon Bun which functions like a Synthesis Pot[4],<br/>but with the added ability to synthesize Cross-Mixing runes like <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/7.png"/></span> Onigiri Payback on a shield.<br/>In addition, Mixergon Buns can be stored inside Preservation Pots, unlike actual Synthesis Pots.<br/><span class="pinkText">Remember that equipment runes and bracelet abilities are disabled during Morphed status.</span></p>

<p><b><u>Eradication Scroll (Blank Scroll)</u></b><br/>Eradicate a monster family that serves as a major threat that you didn't bring a counter to.<br/>Skullmancer when you lack <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/20.png"/></span> Magic Cancel, Nigiri Honcho when you lack <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/17.png"/></span> Nigiriproof,<br/>Bag Knave or Nimble Knave when you don't have an Affixing Bracelet, and so on.<br/>Common route: Bag Knave on 11F → Abyss Dragon on 60F → Okina Monk A on 71F.<br/>Eradication Scroll is easily unlocked and obtained in <a href="/shiren-6/dungeons/domain-of-staves-and-scrolls">Domain of Staves and Scrolls</a>.</p>

<p><b><u>Revival Grass, Invincible Grass</u></b><br/>The player can't request a rescue if they collapse during Thief Mode, so bring 3+ Revival Grass.<br/>Invincible Grass helps keep you safe if the situation deteriorates when stealing.</p>

<p><b><u>Strength Grass x 2</u></b><br/>Being able to equip <span class="oceanBlueText">2</span> bracelets from the start of the run makes things easier,<br/>so do some <a href="/shiren-6/dungeons/serpentcoil-island">Serpentcoil Island</a> runs or such to stock up on Strength Grass beforehand.<br/>There's no need to bring Strength Grass if you main Yamanba's Cleaver & Yamanba's Potlid.</p>

<p><b><u>Porky's Rock</u></b><br/>Simple and space efficient method to deal with all of the Behemoth Monsters you'll encounter.<br/>Buy a Porky Bun at Shukuba Beach → Throw Porky's Rocks in the Warehouse to stock up.</p>

<p><b><u>Sumo Onigiri</u></b><br/>Increases Max Fullness to <span class="oceanBlueText">150</span> if Max Fullness &lt; <span class="oceanBlueText">150</span>, then fully replenishes Fullness.<br/>Sumo status boosts Max HP (<span class="oceanBlueText">+50</span>), Attack Power (<span class="oceanBlueText">x1.5</span>), grants trap immunity and wall dig.<br/>Digging through walls lets you claim buried items and create escape routes for stealing.<br/>Synthesize the <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/29.png"/></span> Sating rune or bring a Bonito Block to remain in Sumo status even longer.<br/>Sumo Onigiri is easily obtained in <a href="/shiren-6/dungeons/sumo-dungeon">Sumo Dungeon</a>.</p>

<p><b><u>Sweet Potato</u></b><br/>Common in shops, and serves as a counter when surrounded by enemies during Sumo status.<br/>Don't forget that Travel Companions inside the room will also warp elsewhere when you eat it.<br/>Some players like to buy any Sweet Potatoes that they see to stock up for other dungeons.</p>

<p><b><u>Mapping Scroll</u></b><br/>All floors have dark hallways, except for 20F which has a Shuffle Dungeon style map.<br/>Save these scrolls for dangerous floors (11F+) instead of reading them without thought,<br/>and you'll get more benefit out of the scroll if you read it at the start of a floor.</p>

<p><b><u>Water-drain Scroll</u></b><br/>Flowing water is pretty common, and foes like Porkon, monks, and Aquatic types appear.<br/>Therefore, it can be nice to keep at least <span class="oceanBlueText">1</span> of these scrolls on hand to counter a bad map.<br/>It costs <span class="oceanBlueText">1,000</span> Gitan, which is the same as Gambler's Scroll - beware if the latter is unidentified.</p>

<p><b><u>Disguising Staff</u></b><br/>Often used to create an early game Cave Mamel (<span class="oceanBlueText">2,000</span> exp), but Pit Mamel doesn't spawn,<br/>and Hat Brat (<span class="oceanBlueText">880</span> exp) spawns on 3-10F, so this is just used as a crowd control option here.<br/>Same high price point as Peach Staff, so it's easily identified in shops.</p>

<p><b><u>Earthmound Staff</u></b><br/>Bury a companion in wall tiles in the corner of a room before stealing from a shop to ensure safety.<br/>The buried companion also serves as a lure that distracts shop-related enemies when detected.<br/>A minimum of <span class="oceanBlueText">2</span> uses is needed to do this, but <span class="oceanBlueText">3</span> is best if Skull Wizard or such is around,<br/>since monsters with ranged attacks can attack through corners.</p>

<p><b><u>Burrowing Staff</u></b><br/>One method that can be used to dig out wall tiles to claim buried items.<br/>Being able to dig <span class="oceanBlueText">10</span> tiles in a single turn can be handy even if you have Sumo status.</p>

<p><b><u>Transmutation Pot</u></b><br/>Insert Gitan to obtain non-Gitan category items like food, equipment, or grass.<br/>One way you might be able to obtain Strength Grass in-dungeon if you forgot to bring some.<br/>Can be reused if you find an Extraction Scroll (<span class="oceanBlueText">1,000</span> Gitan) for sale in a shop.</p>

<p><b><u>Evasive Incense</u></b><br/>Mainly used to counter Porkon (20-30F), Frogginator (31-40F), and Porgon (66-99F).<br/>Also lets you stock up on Porky's Rocks which can be used to one-shot Behemoth Monsters.</p>

<p><b><u>Cautious Incense</u></b><br/>Keeps items/Gitan safe from Ironwalrus (11-30F), Magicwalrus (31-40F), and Froggo types.<br/>Also blocks Bag Knave (11-30F), Nimble Knave (31-50F), and Swordmaster (60-70F) abilities.</p>

<p><b><u>Reflective Incense</u></b><br/>Counters Skullmancer (22-30F), Polygon Singa (11-30F), Hyper Gazer (20-30F), etc.<br/>Can be used to hit yourself with Narrow-escape Staff's magic to instantly warp to the stairs,<br/>but it's risky if foes like Bag Knave (11-30F), Curse Matron (17-30F), or Ironwalrus (11-30F) spawn.</p>

<p><b><u>Gitan</u></b><br/>Consumable items are found in shops in this dungeon, so money is important.<br/>Sacred gear and other rare pieces of equipment generally have higher price tags, too.<br/>However, if you're very confident in your ability to steal from shops, you won't need much Gitan.<br/>Safe ways to amass Gitan include selling Eradication Scrolls from <a href="/shiren-6/dungeons/domain-of-staves-and-scrolls">Domain of Staves and Scrolls</a>,<br/>selling Sumo Onigiri from <a href="/shiren-6/dungeons/sumo-dungeon">Sumo Dungeon</a>, and reading Fixer Scrolls at 0 Gitan in a Warehouse.</p>

<h3 id="travel-companions" class="heading3Pink">Travel Companions</h3>

Travel Companions join at a minimum of Lv15 even when recruited on 1F in this dungeon,<br/>and are naturally immune to Thiefwalrus and Froggo family's theft-related special attacks.

<h4 id="asuka" class="heading4Pink"><img src="../images/npcs/asuka.png"/> Asuka</h4>

Asuka can equip a weapon and shield, so she can end up stronger than Shiren depending on gear.<br/>Aim to also upgrade Asuka's gear on Mixergon (16-30F) floors instead of only focusing on Shiren.<br/>Recommended runes include HP+X, status inflicting, and payback (onigiri and jitter) type runes.<br/>An unidentified weapon or shield can be thrown at Asuka to check upgrade value and cursed state.<br/>This trick isn't as exceptional here since the above can also be checked by looking at prices in shops,<br/>but it's a neat trick for other dungeons where the floor table features more equipment.

<h4 id="tugai" class="heading4Pink"><img src="../images/npcs/tugai.png"/> Tugai</h4>

Tugai has the highest stats among allies, and has an innate chance for critical hits.<br/>He also occasionally takes the hit for the player if they would've collapsed from an attack,<br/>which serves as a means to help boost early game consistency a bit more than usual.<br/>Overall, a simple yet powerful companion who remains useful the entire time.

<h4 id="seki" class="heading4Pink"><img src="../images/npcs/seki.png"/> Seki</h4>

Seki has Swift speed and retreats 1 tile after attacking when space is available.<br/>This combo leads to her shredding through 1vs1 fights, provided the target lacks a ranged attack.<br/>However, keep in mind that she won't retreat if her attack misses - don't get too careless,<br/>like letting her fight Nigiri Boss (11-19F) on her own (Seki misses → gets turned into an onigiri).<br/>Beware of ranged attacker families like Skull Mage, Pullfrog, and Metalhead in particular.

<h4 id="hibiki" class="heading4Pink"><img src="../images/npcs/hibiki.png"/> Hibiki</h4>

Hibiki offers support by using or throwing status altering medicine on herself or at others.<br/>She can boost her own action speed, inflict status conditions on monsters, heal Shiren, etc.<br/>There's some randomness to her ability, but she's no doubt a powerful companion.

<h4 id="porky-the-pirate" class="heading4Pink"><img src="../images/npcs/porky.png"/> Porky the Pirate</h4>

Porky the Pirate can throw Porky's Rocks that deal 20 damage at monsters within a 3 tile radius.<br/>Porky's Rock one-shots Behemoth Monsters, so he serves as a guardian on Behemoth Gate floors.<br/>That said, monster stats ramp up way too quickly for Porky the Pirate to keep up in damage output,<br/>so he's by far the weakest companion in this dungeon once you progress past the first few floors.<br/>He also unfortunately has the lowest Max HP among companions, so he gets taken out easily too.<br/>Basically, expect him to collapse in 2 hits from most monsters from around 11F onwards.

The title of the dungeon only refers to shopping, but of course stealing is always an option.<br/>Use Wall-less Scroll, Reflective Incense & Narrow-escape Staff, Burrowing Staff & Vaulting Staff, etc.<br/>Or bring Trapper's Bracelet → collect and utilize Pitfall Traps to steal with absolutely minimal effort.<br/>It's easy to reach <span class="oceanBlueText">999,999</span> Gitan per run if you repeatedly sell your inventory before stealing it back,<br/>but beware of unidentified Gambler's Scroll (<span class="oceanBlueText">1,000</span> Gitan) since it can reduce your Gitan total to <span class="oceanBlueText">0</span>.

Behemoth Gates are common, so making them vanish for a reward helps replenish items.<br/>It's probably best to stick to early game floors for this, as later floors can get hectic.

# Floor Guide

<h3 id="first-clear" class="heading3Yellow">First Clear</h3>

### General Advice (Bringing Items)

See [Important Items (Bringing Items)](#important-items-(bringing-items)) for some ideas on what to bring.<br/>The essential shield runes are Magic Cancel, Hypnosisproof, Walrusproof, and Nigiriproof.<br/>Affixing Bracelet is needed to protect inventory items against Bag Knave and Nimble Knave.<br/>Wallpass Bracelet can be found in shops in this dungeon - It costs <span class="oceanBlueText">6,500</span> Gitan.<br/>You should be fine for the 1st clear if you have the above + a fully upgraded weapon and shield,<br/>but feel free to bring extra food, Revival Grass, or anything else you'd like just in case.

### 1-2F

<p><span class="purpleText2">Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on 2F.</span><br/>B-Bowboy can catch you off guard if you get lazy about using the <span class="blueText">Look Around</span> command,<br/>but otherwise most other Behemoth Monsters here can only attack when adjacent.<br/>Don't risk a missed rock throw if a Behemoth Monster is adjacent and can act after you,<br/>especially when your Max HP is still very low due to being so early in the dungeon.</p>

Hat Urchin, Death Reaper, and Shagga are the only monsters that spawn on 1-2F.<br/>Enemies are much stronger from 3F, so level up before descending past 2F if your gear is weak.<br/>Most monsters that don't have fixed item drops will drop a Gitan bag when slain in this dungeon,<br/>so lingering until the wind blows (<span class="pinkText">900 Turns</span>) can further supplement Gitan for shopping.<br/>A shop is likely to be generated on most floors, so go ahead and buy food and other useful items<br/>that will help you keep pace with this dungeon's harsher monster table.

Gitan bags found on 1-2F are usually higher in value than later floors, though they can be cursed.<br/>You'll probably be able to secure around <span class="oceanBlueText">3,000</span> Gitan per floor unless your luck is exceptionally bad.<br/>Consider keeping some Gitan bags on hand as projectiles instead of adding them all to your wallet.<br/>Hat Brat (3-10F) requires throwing a Gitan bag worth <span class="oceanBlueText">630+</span> Gitan to one-shot.

### 5-10F

<p><span class="purpleText2">Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on 5-10F.</span></p>

Same as 3-4F, but <span class="pinkText">Skull Wizard</span>, Dartingfrog, and Lv1 monks (except Okina) spawn on 5-10F.<br/>Be careful near Skull Wizard when shopping since it can force you to warp to the stairs,<br/>resulting in Thief Mode activating while you're Paralyzed and pretty much ensuring a collapse.<br/>There's also some risk of a companion becoming Confused and attacking a Shopkeeper.<br/>Tengu Monk D can create swift Skull Wizards, so consider eradicating Skull Mage types<br/>unless you have a Shield of Negation or the <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/20.png"/></span> Magic Cancel or <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/21.png"/></span> Magic Reflect rune.<br/>Eradicating them now also lets you avoid Skullmancer (22-30F) later on.

It's possible to morph into a Hat Urchin type monster to farm items via Skull Wizard's magic,<br/>but going to that much trouble isn't worth it in a dungeon where you can bring items.<br/>Hannya Monk D causes monsters to attack each other and level up or defeat Shopkeepers,<br/>which can be a problem if you're planning on going shopping or encounter a Skull Lord, etc.<br/>Tengu Monk D increases the action speed of other monsters, including leveled up ones.<br/>All said, watch the message log and rush to the stairs if too strong of a foe appears.

Overall, none of these monsters should really be a threat yet if you brought powerful gear.<br/>It'll be hard to progress much further if you're already beginning to struggle.

### 11-14F

<p><span class="purpleText2">Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on 11-14F.</span></p>

The monster lineup features enemies from the 30-60F range of Heart of Serpentcoil Island,<br/>with notables <span class="redText">Nigiri Boss</span> (11-19F), Dragon Pit (11-19F), <span class="pinkText">Polygon Singa</span> (11-30F), <span class="redText">Bag Knave</span> (11-30F),<br/>and some bonus monsters that drop items or Gitan like <span class="lightBlueText">Ironwalrus</span> (11-30F) and <span class="lightBlueText">Froggon</span> (11-30F).<br/>Nigiri Boss is especially dangerous without the <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/17.png"/></span> Nigiriproof rune - use items to avoid Onigiri status,<br/>since a Blast Trap, Rotting Trap, or Blazepuff (13-19F)'s flame can one-shot you as an onigiri.

Dragon Pit (11-19F) has Swift 1 speed so it's difficult to run away, and it also hits pretty hard.<br/>Don't hesitate to use staves before you take damage if your equipment is on the weak side.<br/>It's best to read a Mapping Scroll or equip a Monster-find Bracelet if you decide to linger.

### 15-19F

<p><span class="purpleText2">Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on 15-19F.</span></p>

Lv2 Monks excluding Okina Monk C spawn on 15-19F, so beware of Berserk and Swift foes.<br/>Monks never target other Monk family foes with their abilities, so Paralyzed status is effective<br/>at instantly shutting down Hannya or Tengu monks even if Okame Monk is present on the floor.<br/>Some players like to inflict Sluggish status on Kitsune Monk and let it roam to have it disable<br/>non-monk family monsters on the current floor with its item-morph note.

Monsters start to have higher stats, with Admiral Spearfish (15-30F) being a standout.<br/>This Aquatic type has <span class="greenText">200</span> HP, <span class="orangeText">59</span> attack, and <span class="blueText">50</span> defense, and gets a power boost on water.<br/>It also seeks out a water tile to heal itself when its HP gets low before chasing you again,<br/>meaning things can end up devolving into a quagmire of a fight if your weapon is weak.<br/>Either inflict a status condition or shoot arrows to ensure it collapses - don't let it escape.

Electroid (15-30F) and Popdillo (16-20F) also have <span class="orangeText">50+</span> attack power.<br/>Electroid has Swift 1 speed and always spawns Napping, but Monk notes can wake it up.<br/>Popdillo makes you trip and drop items when adjacent, which can result in pots breaking.<br/>N'dama (15-19F) is trivial to deal with in this dungeon despite normally being a tricky foe,<br/>since it only disguises itself as a Gitan bag and doesn't immediately attack when picked up.<br/>Basically, if you walk on a Gitan bag and it doesn't get auto-added to your wallet, it's a N'dama.

<p><span class="blueText">Mixergon</span> (16-30F) eats and synthesizes up to <span class="oceanBlueText">4</span> thrown items.<br/>However, it has <span class="greenText">118</span> HP, <span class="orangeText">68</span> attack, and <span class="blueText">40</span> defense, and gains power each time it eats an item.<br/>Beware of synthesizing when you have allies - Mixergon (<span class="oceanBlueText">2</span> items eaten) will likely one-shot<br/>Porky the Pirate, and it's also questionable if other characters would survive a single hit.<br/>Mixergon (<span class="oceanBlueText">3</span> items eaten) will likely one-shot any character other than Asuka with good gear.<br/>If your positioning is bad, use Paralyzing Staff or Narrow-escape Staff to adjust as needed.<br/>Also, don't forget that Lv2 Monk abilities will cure Mixergon's Paralyzed status.</p>

Curse Matron (17-30F) curses up to <span class="oceanBlueText">2</span> of your equipped weapon, shield, or bracelets in <span class="oceanBlueText">1</span> turn.<br/>You'll need an item like Fixer Scroll or Exorcism Scroll to remove curses from bracelets.<br/>A cursed weapon and shield isn't a big deal here since Mudster and Mudder don't spawn,<br/>and equipping cursed items prevents Hyper Gazer from being able to wreck your equipment,<br/>if you happen to lack the <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/19.png"/></span> Hypnosisproof shield rune.

### 20-24F

<p><span class="purpleText2">Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on 21-24F (Not 20F).</span></p>

20F is a <span class="goldText">Shuffle Dungeon</span> style floor, meaning the map is chosen from a group of preset patterns.<br/>In this map's case, hallways are never dark, and terrain is often surrounded by water tiles.<br/>Admiral Spearfish (15-30F) can be particularly annoying - Waterwalk and Floating bracelets help.<br/>Use items like Mapping Scroll or Guiding Staff to quickly locate the stairs if possible.

Monks don't spawn between 20-24F, but <span class="pinkText">Porkon</span> (20-30F) appears as a new major threat.<br/>This Beast type monster can throw a <span class="oceanBlueText">20</span> damage Porky's Rock anywhere within a <span class="oceanBlueText">8</span> tile radius,<br/>which can be extremely irritating when paired with a floor that has flowing water and rafts.<br/>If the map's layout is complex, consider rushing to the stairs instead of exploring for items.<br/>Porkon has <span class="greenText">150</span> HP, <span class="orangeText">75</span> attack, and <span class="blueText">50</span> defense, so also be careful when trading hits.<br/>New additions also include Lashagga (20-24F), Megataur (20-30F), and Great Samurai (20-30F).

Other trickier foes include Hyper Gazer (20-30F), Fleefrog (20-30F), and <span class="pinkText">Skullmancer</span> (22-30F).<br/>Hyper Gazer hypnotizes you when adjacent, but the <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/19.png"/></span> Hypnosisproof shield rune blocks it.<br/>Skullmancer's magic inflicts Paralyzed, Sluggish, Sealed, Onigiri, Disguised (Confused), or Lv-1.<br/>Skull Mage family monsters have a <span class="oceanBlueText">100%</span> special attack usage rate when lined up at a distance.<br/>If you don't have the <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/20.png"/></span> Magic Cancel shield rune, it's best to throw an Eradication Scroll at it.<br/>Fleefrog pulls you and attacks from up to <span class="oceanBlueText">10</span> tiles away, but Sumo status negates the pull effect.

<h3 id="general-advice-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Red">General Advice (Bringing Items)</h3>

The extension is an additional <span class="oceanBlueText">59</span> floors, so plan what items to bring in accordingly.<br/>Powerful equipment goes without saying - don't skip out on synthesizing key runes beforehand.<br/>Staves with 20-50+ uses are nice against single-targets, and are great inventory space savers.<br/>Shops don't have special merchandise near the end, unlike [Clash of Weapons and Shields](/dungeons/clash-of-weapons-and-shields),<br/>so this extension is not the right place if you're looking for special rewards.

Notable extension spawns include Okina Monk A and Tengu Monk A appearing on 50-99F,<br/>and monsters potentially capable of forcing Thief Mode to trigger appearing between 76-99F.<br/>Nimble Knave & Nigiri Honcho (31-50F) and Swordmaster (60-70F) are the threats to items.<br/>Deathead (41-70F), Abyss Dragon (60-70F), and Porgon (66-99F) are the ranged attackers.<br/>Lastly, special monsters spawn between 50-90F (X0F = Ultra Gazer, X5F = Gitan Mamel).

<h3 id="41-99f" class="heading3Red">41-99F</h3>

<p><span class="purpleText2">Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on 41-98F.</span></p>

Lv4 Monks appear in various combinations between 41-99F.<br/><span class="orangeText">Okame</span>, <span class="pinkText">Hannya</span>, and <span class="orangeText">Kitsune</span> spawn on 41-60F, while <span class="redText">Okina</span> and <span class="redText">Tengu</span> spawn on 50-99F.<br/>Detonachin (60-80F) can vaporize merchandise, but Bowboy & Pop Tank monsters don't appear.<br/>Special foes appear every 5 floors starting from 50F (X0F = <span class="redText">Ultra Gazer</span>, X5F = <span class="redText">Gitan Mamel</span>).<br/>Ultra Gazer can hypnotize the player even when they're in a wall, so don't let your guard down.<br/>The best counter is to rely on the <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/19.png"/></span> Hypnosisproof shield rune to block its special attack.

Other notable threats enter or cycle out of the monster table every 5-20 floors or so.<br/>40-49F - <span class="redText">Nimble Knave</span>, <span class="redText">Nigiri Honcho</span>, <span class="pinkText">Miracle Hen</span>, and Lv4 Ninjas.<br/>50-59F - Mostly Lv4 Ninjas and Lv4 Monks, so this is one of the milder floor ranges.<br/>60-65F - <span class="redText">Swordmaster</span> (60-70F) and <span class="redText">Abyss Dragon</span> (60-70F) are the 2 major threats.<br/>66-99F - <span class="redText">Porgon</span> (66-99F), <span class="orangeText">Grampadillo</span> (76-99F), <span class="pinkText">Polygon Stunna</span> (81-95F), <span class="pinkText">Tiger Launcher</span> (86-99F).

Grampadillo or Tiger Launcher can send you out of a shop, forcing Thief Mode to trigger.<br/>The main counter is to activate Sumo status → avoid letting Polygon Stunna lower Max Fullness.<br/>It's also recommended to throw an Eradication Scroll at Okina Monk A for game tempo reasons.<br/>Wallpass Bracelet is handy when it comes to avoiding monk-boosted shop-related enemies.<br/>Unlike some other dungeons, there's nothing special about merchandise in shops near the end.
