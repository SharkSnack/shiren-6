# Strategy

<h3 id="general-advice-(first-clear)" class="heading3Yellow">General Advice (First Clear)</h3>

This dungeon is trivial if you bring powerful items and bracelets that counter specific enemies.<br/>The difficulty is perhaps above [Dune of Batsu](/dungeons/dune-of-batsu), since stronger monsters appear earlier on.<br/>Threats include Fencer (6-11F), Terror Scorpion (6-9F), Mudster (6-11F), and Curse Matron (14-20F).<br/>Reach 10 strength and equip Monster-find + Cleansing / Rustproof / Affixing / Cursebreak bracelets,<br/>switching the second bracelet out depending on the opponent (or bring some Sealing Staves).

Cannons lack power against most non-behemoth monsters, and can destroy items on the ground.<br/>Some foes prioritize manning a cannon instead of chasing Shiren, making it an effective distraction.<br/>For flowing water and raft tiles, aim to go with the flow of water instead of against it if possible.<br/>Otherwise, you'll probably end up having to fight wave after wave of raft-riding monsters.<br/>Wishing Shrine, Fever Time, and Kron's Challenge can provide great benefits, although random.<br/>Be sure to bring items that let you dig out wall tiles, along with an item like Item-find Bracelet.

Bouncy Walls are pretty common and Bouncy Bracelet can be found on the ground in this dungeon,<br/>so items can be duplicated easily in bouncy wall rooms using that bracelet and an Earthmound Staff.<br/>The trick is to make the thrown item bounce off of all 4 sides of the Bouncy Wall Room.

Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on all floors except for 1F, 10F, and 20F.<br/>It's fine to try for the item drop at first, but consider skipping it once tougher enemies appear.<br/>The Behemoth Monster lineup remains the same throughout the entire dungeon.

<h3 id="dungeon-gimmicks" class="heading3Yellow">Dungeon Gimmicks</h3>

This section is just an overview - See [Dungeon Gimmicks](/system/gameplay-basics#dungeon-gimmicks) for complete details.

<h4 id="cannons" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/cannon_1.png"/> Cannons</h4>

<p><span class="purpleText">Cannons</span> are stationary objects that can be interacted with inside a room within a dungeon.<br/>Perform a normal attack towards a cannon to fire it in the direction you attacked in.<br/>Cannons deal fixed damage, break after 5 uses, and can destroy items depending on type.<br/>They lack power to defeat tougher enemies, but can be used to slay Behemoth Monsters.<br/>Certain monster families will prioritize manning a cannon instead of chasing Shiren.</p>

<h4 id="behemoth-gates" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/behemoth_gate.png"/> Behemoth Gate</h4>

<p><span class="purpleText">Behemoth Gates</span> spawn larger (3x3), slower versions of monsters that have far greater attack power.<br/>These foes are called <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/other/behemoth_icon.png"/></span><span class="purpleText"> Behemoth Monsters</span>, and have 1 HP aside from Explochin types who have 3 HP.<br/>A single rock or projectile is enough to one-shot most types, but they come with a <span class="purpleText">Behemoth Barrier</span><br/>which protects the Behemoth Monster from most attacks coming from the front or side of the Behemoth.<br/>Rocks arc over barriers, and attacks with piercing properties like Silver Arrows or Piercing Cannons<br/>can be used to successfully strike Behemoth Monsters through their barrier.</p>

Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on all floors except for 1F, 10F, and 20F here.<br/>The tricky Behemoths are Death Reaper (normal speed), Bashagga (4 attacks), and Bowboy (ranged).<br/>Otherwise, none of the other Behemoth Monsters should be notably dangerous to deal with,<br/>provided you make use of items like Porky's Rocks or objects like Piercing Cannons.

<h4 id="bouncy-wall-room" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/bouncy_wall.png"/> Bouncy Wall Room</h4>

A room containing special blue wall tiles that reflect thrown items back at the player.<br/>Throw an item at a wall diagonally and have it bounce off of all 4 sides to duplicate the item.<br/>The walls have a decent chance to break each time an item is successfully duplicated,<br/>and are guaranteed to break after 10 successful item duplications on the current floor.<br/>The duplicated item is a plain (non-upgraded, non-sacred, non-blessed) version of the thrown item,<br/>and the item's capacity or quantity is within standard range of what you'd find on the ground.<br/>Bouncy Bracelet + Earthmound Staff can make some unsolvable rooms solvable.

<h4 id="flowing-water" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/flowing_water.png"/> Flowing Water</h4>

A 1-tile wide stream that ends with a whirlpool that warps you back to the start of the stream.<br/>Raft tiles (1x1) that often have items or monsters riding them frequently flow downstream,<br/>so exploring against the current can result in what feels like an endless horde of enemies.<br/>The player can step on a raft to cross to the other side, or ride a raft downstream as well,<br/>and most monsters without ranged attacks step off of a raft when they spot the player.

<h4 id="wishing-shrine" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/wishing_shrine.png"/> Wishing Shrine</h4>

Object offering 3 wishes, and a <span class="purpleText">Gate of Wishes</span> leading to <span class="purpleText">Tunnel of Wishes</span> opens after a selection.<br/>Tunnel of Wishes acts as a bonus zone where the item(s) you wished for can be found on the ground,<br/>and the monster table inside Tunnel of Wishes is identical to the floor you were on before entering.<br/>Wishing Shrines are usually hidden inside a wall tile (shiny or non-shiny), buried room, or Gitan vault.<br/>A chime will sound when a Wishing Shrine is present on a floor if a Shield of Presage is equipped.<br/>One <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/monsters_s/47-1.png"/></span> Golden Egg Thing is always generated when a Wishing Shrine is present on a floor.

<p><b><u><span class="peachText">Possible Wishes</span>:</u></b></p>

- Item Category
    - Weapon, Shield, Bracelet, Arrow / Rock, Food, Scroll, Staff, Incense, Pot, or Gitan.
- Specific Item
    - Preservation Pot, Revival Grass, Blank Scroll, Fixer Scroll, Wallpass Bracelet,<br/>Waterwalk Bracelet, Clairvoyant Bracelet, Monster-find Bracelet, or Item-find Bracelet.
- Miscellaneous
    - Identify Items - Identifier Scroll, Identifier Pot, etc.
    - Dig Through Walls - Pickaxe, Burrowing Staff, etc.
    - Increase Strength - Strength Grass, Strength Bracelet, etc.
    - Level Up - Fortune Grass, Angel Seed, etc.
    - Destroy Traps - Pickaxe, Wooden Mallet, Burrowing Staff, Trapseeker, Seewell Grass, etc.
    - Upgrade - Heavenly Scroll, Earthly Scroll, Plating Scroll, Slot-adding Scroll,<br/>Rune-eraser Scroll, Silverpurge Scroll, Upgrading Pot, etc.

<h4 id="kron's-challenge-&-fever-time" class="heading4Yellow">Kron's Challenge & Fever Time</h4>

<p><b><u><span class="peachText">Kron's Challenge</span>:</u></b></p>

Challenges that can occur at random in select postgame dungeons.<br/>It begins at the start of a floor, and the player is rewarded if they complete the challenge.<br/>The player is still rewarded based on their progress if they advance floors before the challenge ends,<br/>but the player usually won't be rewarded if they advance using a Pitfall or Floor-exiting trap.<br/>See [Kron's Challenge](/system/gameplay-basics#kron's-challenge) for possible challenges and rewards.

<p><b><u><span class="peachText">Fever Time</span>:</u></b></p>

Real and limited-time events that can occur at random in select postgame dungeons.<br/>When it does randomly trigger, it usually does so when the player is in a room instead of a hallway.<br/>There's no penalty for ignoring the event similar to Kron's Challenge, so don't feel pressured to act quickly.<br/>You can easily end up getting hit by a nasty special attack or stepping on a trap when in a hurry.<br/>See [Fever Time](/system/gameplay-basics#possible-fever-times) for possible fever times and rewards.

<h4 id="weapon-&-shield" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/weapon/37.png"/> <img src="../images/shield/32.png"/> Weapon & Shield</h4>

<p><b><u>Hunky-dory Shield (<span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/28.png"/></span> Full Armor)</u></b><br/>The Full Armor shield rune halves damage received when Shiren's current HP = max HP.<br/>In this dungeon, it mostly serves as added protection against enemy cannons when Shiren is Lv1.<br/>Runes like <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/48.png"/></span> HP +20 which increase max HP are also great for early game survival.</p>

<p><b><u>Yamanba's Cleaver & Yamanba's Potlid (or 2 Strength Grass)</u></b><br/>Methods to start the dungeon with 10+ strength, allowing for 2 bracelets to be equipped.<br/>Don't forget to bring a Rejuvenation Pot if you choose to go with Strength Grass,<br/>since a Vile Scorpion (3-5F) or Poison Arrow Trap can lower your strength.<br/>Alternatively, just keep Cleansing Bracelet equipped at all times.</p>

<p><b><u>Peach Club (<span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/weapon/27.png"/></span> Peach Bun) or Peach Staff</u></b><br/>Turn Soul Reaper (10-20F) or King Reaper (21-50F) into a Peach Bun to gain wallpass and swift speed.<br/>Eating a Peach Bun cures Far-throwing status, so it's nice on Swordsman (6-11F, 21-50F) family floors.<br/>Don't forget that Hoppin' Batter (4-7F, 13-17F, 30-50F) family monsters nullify or reflect magic bullets.</p>

<h4 id="bracelets" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/bracelet.png"/> Bracelets</h4>

<p><b><u>Monster-find Bracelet</u></b><br/>Hallways are visible so it's not essential, but it's nice to detect nearby off-screen monsters.<br/>Handy for Swordsman (6-11F, 21-50F) and Mudkin (6-11F, 21-50F) family floors in particular.</p>

<h3 id="farming-techniques" class="heading3Green">Farming Techniques</h3>

None of these techniques are necessary since it's a dungeon that allows carry-in items.

<h4 id="igneous-hunting-(3-5f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters_s/41-3.png"/> Igneous Hunting (3-5F)</h4>

Fierous types level up when hit by flames, and Blazeous (3-5F) & Flamepuff (3-5F) overlap.<br/>Easiest methods: Peach Club (<span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/weapon/27.png"/></span> Peach Bun), Peach Staff, or Kajin Fuuma (<span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/weapon/23.png"/></span> Flame Shot).<br/>Hit a Blazeous (Lv2) with fire damage using Flamepuff Bun or Flame Shot, then slay it for <span class="oceanBlueText">999</span> exp.<br/>Igneous (Lv3) has <span class="oceanBlueText">165</span> HP and <span class="oceanBlueText">56</span> attack, so you'll probably want a shield strength of at least <span class="oceanBlueText">60</span>.<br/>Infernous (Lv4) provides <span class="oceanBlueText">1,999</span> exp, but might be too tough with <span class="oceanBlueText">285</span> HP and <span class="oceanBlueText">69</span> attack.

<h4 id="mudster-/-mudder-hunting-(6-11f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/32-3.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/32-4.png"/> Mudster / Mudder Hunting (6-11F)</h4>

Unequip your weapon and shield → punch Lv3 Mudsters (or Lv4 Mudders) to make them multiply.<br/>Seal off the stairs room using Paralyzing or Earthmound staves, and stand on the stairs.<br/>It's recommended to bring a Fortune Staff or such if you want to hunt Lv4 Mudders.<br/>Mudster provides <span class="oceanBlueText">460</span> experience points, and Mudder <span class="oceanBlueText">920</span> experience points.

<h4 id="chow-bun-(6-20f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters_s/48-1.png"/> Chow Bun (6-20F)</h4>

Chow appears on 6-20F, so stock up on Chow Buns for Sacred Item farming in Isle of the Mighty.<br/>However, this is mainly only notable for players who haven't purchased the DLC.<br/>See [Isle of the Mighty](/dungeons/isle-of-the-mighty) for details.

# Floor Guide

<h3 id="first-clear" class="heading3Yellow">First Clear</h3>

### General Advice (Bringing Items)

An upgraded weapon and shield & Cleansing + Affixing + Rustproof bracelets nearly ensures a win.<br/>The only threats against strong gear are Lv2-3 scorpions (3-9F), Fencer (6-11F), and Mudster (6-11F).<br/>Curse Matron (14-20F) curses your equipped weapon, shield, or <span class="emphasis">bracelets</span>, but it's not bad here,<br/>since Curse Matron doesn't overlap with Scorpion, Swordsman, or Mudkin family monsters.<br/><span class="orangeText">※ Returning to Shukuba Beach auto-removes all curses from inventory items.</span>

### 1-2F

<p><span class="purpleText2">Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on 2F.</span><br/>B-Bowboy can catch you off guard if you get lazy about using the <span class="blueText">Look Around</span> command,<br/>but otherwise most other Behemoth Monsters here can only attack when adjacent.<br/>Don't risk a missed rock throw if a Behemoth Monster is adjacent and can act after you,<br/>especially when your Max HP is still very low due to being so early in the dungeon.</p>

Regular cannons can one-shot you by dealing 20 damage without the <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/11.png"/></span> Anti-Blast rune,<br/>so linger in the starting room until Max HP reaches 21+ if you lack <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/28.png"/></span> Full Armor, <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/48.png"/></span> HP +20, etc.<br/>Shagga, Octopling, Hat Urchin, and Death Reaper are the only monsters that spawn on 1-2F.<br/>None of them are a threat with strong gear, but level up a bit before descending to 3F.

<h3 id="sacred-tree-extension" class="heading3Pink">Sacred Tree Extension</h3>

<h3 id="general-advice-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Red">General Advice (Bringing Items)</h3>

Unlike the first clear, you'll want to avoid getting bracelets cursed by Curse Matron (14-20F),<br/>since Demon Scorpion (21-39F), Battler (21-34F), Swordmaster (35-50F), and Mudder (21-50F) overlap.<br/>Cursenior (21-50F) also appears, so expect to constantly have to swap around equipped bracelets.<br/>The bracelets that counter these monsters are Cleansing, Affixing, Rustproof, and Cursebreak.<br/>Eradication Scroll knocks out one of the major threats, making things slightly less tedious.

Some players like to keep Wallpass Bracelet equipped instead of swapping bracelets,<br/>but Adamant Tank (21-39) and Steadfast Tank (40-50F) can expose you even if you're inside a wall,<br/>so the Wallpass Bracelet route isn't recommended over properly countering the threats.

Tiger Launcher (21-50F), Showpin (21-50F), and Okina Monk A (31-39F) can cause accidents,<br/>since their abilities either force enemies to become adjacent or summon additional foes.<br/>Extreme threats like Abyss Dragon and Frenzy Radish don't appear at all, thankfully.

<h3 id="1-20f" class="heading3Red">1-20F</h3>

<p><span class="purpleText2">Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on 2-9F and 11-19F.</span><br/>Dangerous Behemoths include B-Death Reaper, B-Gigataur, B-Lashagga, B-Bashagga, and B-Bowboy.<br/>When it doubt, use the <span class="blueText">Look Around</span> command to check if a distant Behemoth is B-Bowboy.</p>

Same advice as the [first clear](#first-clear), but avoid getting bracelets cursed by Curse Matron (14-20F).<br/>Beware of cannons at the start if you lack <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/28.png"/></span> Full Armor, <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/11.png"/></span> Anti-Blast, <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/10.png"/></span> Anti-Dragon, <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/48.png"/></span> HP +20, etc.<br/>Go ahead and eat 2 Strength Grass (or otherwise activate the ability to equip 2 bracelets) right away,<br/>since it will be a significant help between 6-11F and later on between 21-50F.

<h3 id="21-50f" class="heading3Red">21-50F</h3>

<p><span class="purpleText2">Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on 21-50F.</span><br/>That's right, Behemoth Gates can spawn even on the final floor, unlike most other dungeons.<br/>The Behemoth Monster lineup is the same, so B-Bowboy is still the only notable ranged attacker.</p>

The <span class="pinkText">Danger Zone</span> of the Sacred Tree Extension, for similar reasons to 6-11F during the 1st clear.<br/>Most monsters in this floor range have 200+ HP and maybe an average of 65-70+ Attack Power,<br/>but these extension floors still shouldn't be too bad if you have a fully upgraded weapon and shield.<br/>Demon Scorpion (21-39F), Lv3-4 Swordsman (21-50F), Mudder (21-50F), and Cursenior (21-50F) overlap,<br/>and Tiger Launcher (21-50F), Showpin (21-50F), and Okina Monk A (31-39F) can complicate situations.<br/>Adamant Tank (21-39) and Steadfast Tank (40-50F) shoot cannonballs even if you're in a wall tile.

The best Eradication Scroll target includes Scorpion, Swordsman, Mudkin, or Curse Girl families,<br/>since it means there will be one fewer monster families you need to swap bracelets around for.<br/>Swap between Cleansing, Affixing, Rustproof, and Cursebreak bracelets to counter the above.<br/>Sumo Onigiri can be used to counter Tiger Launcher, but Showpin and Okina Monk A are trickier.<br/>Side note, but this is also a rare postgame dungeon where the final floor's BGM isn't the same as 1F.
