# Strategy

<h3 id="general-advice-(first-clear-&-sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Yellow">General Advice (First Clear & Sacred Tree Extension)</h3>

The first clear is <span class="oceanBlueText">30</span> floors, while the Sacred Tree Extension is <span class="oceanBlueText">99</span> floors.<br/>Hallways are fully visible on 1-30F, then alternate between visible and dark on 31-99F.<br/>A Behemoth Gate is generated on all floors, and regular monsters generally don't spawn.<br/>The basic strategy is to collect items until about 10F, then switch to rushing to the stairs.<br/>For the Sacred Tree Extension, the item collection range can be extended to 59F or so.

If you find a Bouncy Wall room, prioritize duplicating items like Revival Grass or Blank Scroll,<br/>and check to make sure Far-throwing Bracelet isn't equipped before throwing an item.<br/>Floor layouts become more complex with depth, so save Pickaxes and Burrowing Staves.<br/>Items that replenish Fullness are also quite important, since floors are generally large.<br/>Lastly, Fever Time should probably be ignored unless you're desperate for items.

<h4 id="behemoth-monsters" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/behemoth_icon.png"/> Behemoth Monsters</h4>

Most Behemoths have Slow speed since action speed for Behemoths is reduced by 1 stage,<br/>but the Sacred Tree Extension features select <span class="pinkText">Normal</span> or <span class="pinkText">Swift 1</span> speed Behemoths later on.<br/>Pumphantasm attacks through walls, and ranged attackers like Bowboy one-shot you from afar,<br/>so keep track of turn order and consider running away before you become cornered.

Behemoths can be instantly defeated by projectiles (rocks, arrows, thrown items) or cannons,<br/>but the player must aim for a spot that isn't protected by a Behemoth Barrier to deal damage.<br/>Rocks or Far-throwing Bracelet (or Silver Arrow) can be used to bypass a Behemoth Barrier.<br/>Slaying every Behemoth you encounter ensures safety of course, but don't run out of items.<br/>It's also possible to slay a Behemoth with a normal attack or such by striking it from behind,<br/>which makes Switching Staff (effective even against barriers) an option when cornered.

<h4 id="effectiveness-changes-vs.-behemoths" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> Effectiveness Changes vs. Behemoths</h4>

- Staves
    - Always immune
        - Peach, Disguising, Knockback
    - Effective even against barrier-protected tiles
        - Switching, Vaulting, Narrow-escape (× Paralyzed), Skull Mage's (warp), Unlucky, Fortune
    - Effective only against non-barrier tiles
        - All other status condition or damage inflicting staves.
    - Earthmound Staff Note
        - Breakable wall tiles can't be used to block a Behemoth's pursuit.
- Scrolls
    - Gathering Scroll doesn't attract Behemoth Monsters.
    - Behemoths are immune to Fixer Scroll's Paralyzed status effect.
    - Windblade Scroll isn't effective in hallways, but is effective in rooms.
    - Sanctuary Scroll still prevents adjacent Behemoths from using normal attacks.
    - Eradication Scroll slays a Behemoth, but doesn't prevent it from spawning again.
- Peach Buns
    - Behemoths are completely immune to a thrown Peach Bun's morphing effect.
- Cannons
    - Every cannon type except for <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/other/peach_bun_cannon.png"/></span> Peach Bun Cannon is effective.
    - The <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/other/piercing_cannon_1.png"/></span> Piercing Cannon series can slay Behemoths from any direction.
    - The <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/other/cannon_1.png"/></span> Cannon series must be fired in a way where the blast hits a non-barrier tile.
    - Cannon objects can be pushed onto a room entrance tile to block a Behemoth's pursuit.
    - Behemoth Monsters never operate cannon objects.

<h4 id="behemoth-spawns" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/behemoth_gate.png"/> Behemoth Spawns</h4>

Behemoths despawn over time, but the deeper you go the longer they linger before vanishing.<br/>You might only see 2 spawn early on, but by the end upwards of 5\~10 can spawn at once.<br/>The Behemoth Gate also seems to change locations faster on certain floors in this dungeon,<br/>so Behemoth Monsters can be surprisingly spread out instead of clustering in one spot.

The Behemoth Gate vanishes and leaves behind an item after you enter every room on the floor.<br/>This is a nice way to obtain useful items during early\~mid game since maps are simpler at first.<br/>All Behemoth Monsters present on the floor vanish when the Behemoth Gate vanishes.<br/>The best time to identify items is after you've made the Behemoth Gate vanish on the floor.<br/>Synthesis Pot seems to be a Behemoth Gate vanishing reward exclusive item here.

<h4 id="non-behemoth-spawns" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/monsters/14-4.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/11-4.png"/> Non-Behemoth Spawns</h4>

<p><span class="redText">Bashagga</span> and <span class="redText">Gigataur</span> don't spawn as turns elapse, but can be hidden in breakable wall tiles.<br/>They also appear when a Monster Pot, Monstercall Scroll, or Schubell family ability gets used,<br/>so it's best to avoid hitting sparkling walls or pillars, and don't use the listed items.<br/>Schubell types use their ability even from a distance if the player is within view.</p>

<h3 id="important-items-(first-clear-&-sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Purple">Important Items (First Clear & Sacred Tree Extension)</h3>

<h4 id="weapon-&-shield-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/weapon/31.png"/> <img src="../images/shield/26.png"/> Weapon & Shield</h4>

<p><b><u>Pickaxe</u></b><br/>Escape from a dead end when you're at risk of being cornered.<br/>Can be easier to utilize if you also have a Waterwalk Bracelet equipped.</p>

<p><b><u>Kama Itachi</u></b><br/>Attack 3 forward directions at the same time.<br/>Useful when hunting Bashagga & Gigataur using Monstercall Scroll + Sanctuary Scroll.</p>

<p><b><u>Shield of Sating</u></b><br/>Low defense, but doubles the number of turns you can act before starving.<br/>It's fine to main this shield during the 1st clear, but synthesize it during the 2nd clear.</p>

<p><b><u>Watchful Shield, Kappa's Dish, Gusty Shield</u></b><br/>Chance-based defensive shields that potentially let you conserve Revival Grass.<br/>Gusty Shield (Guts rune) grants a <span class="oceanBlueText">30%</span> chance to survive a fatal hit with <span class="oceanBlueText">1</span> HP remaining,<br/>and considering most attacks one-shot you here, it's fantastic in this dungeon.</p>

<h4 id="offensive-items" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/rock.png"/> Offensive Items</h4>

<p><b><u>Rock</u></b> / <b><u>Porky's Rock</u></b> / <b><u>Silver Arrow</u></b><br/>Items that can be used to defeat a Behemoth Monster from the front.<br/>Keep in mind that they can miss, and save them for later floors if possible.</p>

<p><b><u>Far-throwing Bracelet</u></b><br/>Shoot or throw non-silver arrows, equipment, or Gitan to slay Behemoths from the front.<br/>The item can't be reclaimed if you miss, so you'll burn through more items while it's equipped.<br/>Even more effective if you make it a point to collect lots of arrows from various arrow traps.<br/>Throw unneeded items outside the map when Bored Kappa family Behemoths are around.<br/>Don't forget to unequip it when throwing an item at a Bouncy Wall.</p>

<h4 id="movement-items" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> Exploration Items</h4>

<p><b><u>Monster-find Bracelet</u></b><br/>Makes it safer to explore rooms by revealing monster positions.<br/>Particularly nice to have for floors where B-Pumphantasm types spawn (1-10, 21-30F, 50-99F).</p>

<p><b><u>Waterwalk Bracelet</u></b><br/>Avoid having to take long detours due to waterways.<br/>Food in your inventory that's outside of a pot will rot while walking on water.</p>

<p><b><u>Water-drain Scroll</u></b><br/>Avoid having to take long detours due to waterways.</p>

<p><b><u>Burrowing Staff</u></b><br/>Create alternate paths to rooms, or escape from a dead end.</p>

<p><b><u>Vaulting Staff</u></b><br/>Escape from a dangerous situation, or skip across waterways.</p>

<p><b><u>Switching Staff</u></b><br/>Switch positions with a Behemoth Monster while in a hallway, then shoot them from behind.</p>

<p><b><u>Guiding Staff</u></b><br/>Reveals the location of the stairs on the map.<br/>Can be used alongside Blurry Incense to safely rush stairs near the end.</p>

<p><b><u>Mapping Scroll</u></b><br/>Use these closer to the end where floors become generally larger and more complex.</p>

<h4 id="survival-/-other-items" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/food.png"/> Survival / Other Items</h4>

<p><b><u>Preservation Pot</u></b><br/>Great to have to prevent onigiri from rotting.<br/>Especially nice if you happen to obtain a Sumo Onigiri from a Behemoth Gate.</p>

<p><b><u>Sumo Onigiri</u></b><br/>Counters Starvation Traps that reduce Max Fullness by <span class="oceanBlueText">25</span> per activation.<br/>Beware that you can't use movement staves like Vaulting Staff during Sumo status.<br/>Only obtainable by making a Behemoth Gate vanish.</p>

<p><b><u>Special Onigiri</u></b><br/>Eat it at the start of a floor to try for Satiated status, or before descending for Knowledgeable status.<br/>Don't forget to regen HP until full beforehand, or else you'll just get the HP restoring effect.</p>

<p><b><u>Leaping Grass</u></b><br/>Escape from a dead end situation, but doesn't work during Sumo status.<br/>It's probably best to try and keep at least <span class="oceanBlueText">1</span> on hand at all times.</p>

<p><b><u>Invincible Grass, Revival Grass</u></b><br/>Revival Grass counters unexpected hits and starvation, Invincible Grass helps when sandwiched.<br/>Either of these grasses are occasionally obtained when making a Behemoth Gate vanish,<br/>and should be considered for duplication when you see a Bouncy Wall Room.</p>

<p><b><u>Wall-less Scroll</u></b><br/>Immediately makes the Behemoth Gate vanish due to the floor changing to a single room.<br/>However, Bashaggas and Gigataurs can be hidden inside walls as penalty monsters.<br/>It's best to pair it with movement related staves to evade said enemies.</p>

<p><b><u>Blank Scroll</u></b><br/>Can be found on the floor, and also obtained by making a Behemoth Gate vanish.<br/>Good writing options: Mapping, Wall-less (erase Behemoth Gate), Fixer (replenish Fullness), etc.<br/>Always keep <span class="oceanBlueText">1</span> on hand for Bouncy Wall Room duplication purposes.</p>

<p><b><u>Blurry Incense</u></b><br/>The player can still detect nearby Behemoth Monsters during this incense's effect.<br/>However, keep in mind that it makes it harder to locate the stairs.</p>

<p><b><u>Earthmound Staff</u></b><br/>Swing it while facing a water tile to create a pillar → break the pillar to turn it into dry ground.<br/>Behemoth Monsters destroy pillars, so this can't be used to block their pursuit.</p>

<p><b><u>Monster Pot</u></b><br/><span class="pinkText">Extremely dangerous, since you'll become surrounded by Bashaggas and Gigataurs.</span><br/>Always use <span class="blueText">Tap</span> type pots while standing on the stairs until this is identified,<br/>unless the pot was obtained by making a Behemoth Gate vanish.</p>

<p><b><u>Far-throwing Trap</u></b><br/>Shoot or throw non-silver arrows, equipment, or Gitan to slay Behemoths from the front.<br/>The item can't be reclaimed if you miss, so you'll burn through more items with this effect.<br/>You also won't be able to duplicate items using a Bouncy Wall with this status.</p>

<h3 id="farming-techniques" class="heading3Green">Farming Techniques</h3>

<h4 id="leveling-up" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/other/piercing_cannon_1.png"/> Leveling Up</h4>

If you find a cannon in a room early on, use it to hunt some Behemoth Monsters to level up.<br/><span class="orangeText2">11-20F</span> can spawn Behemoths that deal a fixed <span class="oceanBlueText">20</span> damage, so <span class="oceanBlueText">21</span>, <span class="oceanBlueText">41</span>, or <span class="oceanBlueText">61+</span> Max HP is safer.<br/>Also, Archdragon (60-80F) deals <span class="oceanBlueText">30</span> damage, and Abyss Dragon (81-99F) deals <span class="oceanBlueText">40</span> damage,<br/>so aim to reach at least <span class="oceanBlueText">31+</span> Max HP by <span class="orangeText2">60F</span>, and <span class="oceanBlueText">41+</span> Max HP by <span class="orangeText2">81F</span> for the 2nd clear.

Some players like to hunt Bashagga or Gigataur for experience points using specific items.<br/>This is more useful for the 2nd clear since Abyss Dragon can spawn, but it's not mandatory.<br/>Gigataur has <span class="greenText">235</span> HP and grants <span class="oceanBlueText">1,790</span> exp, while Bashagga has <span class="greenText">154</span> HP and grants <span class="oceanBlueText">950</span> exp.<br/>You'll need <span class="oceanBlueText">2</span> Blank Scrolls (Monstercall Scroll + Sanctuary Scroll) to ensure safety,<br/>and be careful not to get sent outside of the dungeon by wind (<span class="pinkText">900 Turns</span>).

<h4 id="duplicate-items-using-bouncy-walls" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/other/bouncy_wall.png"/> Duplicate Items Using Bouncy Walls</h4>

Bouncy Wall Rooms are rooms containing blue walls that reflect thrown items back at the player.<br/>Throw an item at a wall diagonally and have it bounce off of all <span class="oceanBlueText">4</span> sides to duplicate the item.<br/>The walls have a decent chance to break each time an item is successfully duplicated,<br/>and are guaranteed to break after <span class="oceanBlueText">10</span> successful item duplications on the current floor.<br/>The duplicated item is a plain (non-upgraded/sacred/blessed) version of the thrown item,<br/>and the item's capacity or quantity is within standard range of what you'd find on the ground.<br/>Some Bouncy Wall room layouts are unsolvable.

# Floor Guide

<h3 id="first-clear" class="heading3Yellow">First Clear</h3>

### General Advice

See [General Advice (First Clear & Sacred Tree Extension)](#general-advice-(first-clear-&-sacred-tree-extension)) for an overview.<br/>The basics are <span class="purpleText2">Explore without being detected</span> and <span class="purpleText2">Pierce barriers to slay Behemoths</span>.<br/>The former is self-explanatory, but avoiding detection by behemoths helps with survivability.<br/>However, the Pumphantasm family detects you from anywhere on the floor even as Behemoths.<br/>The latter means find ways to pierce or bypass barriers to slay a Behemoth from any direction,<br/>which primarily include using items like Rocks, Silver Arrows, and Far-throwing Bracelet.<br/>Switching Staff can be used to get behind a Behemoth that's facing you for a back-strike,<br/>but always keep track of turn order and remember that normal attacks can miss.

### 1-10F

Beware of B-Pumphantasm since it has the ability to pass through walls.<br/>If you find a cannon in a room, use it to hunt Behemoths to level up (aim for <span class="oceanBlueText">21+</span> Max HP).<br/>It's good to step into all rooms to make the Behemoth Gate vanish for an item drop early on,<br/>but weigh the risk of starvation if you step on a Hunger Trap or begin to run low on food.<br/>Fixer Scroll (read when starving) or Revival Grass (revive) can replenish Fullness if needed,<br/>so consider duplicating these (or a Large Onigiri) in a Bouncy Wall Room when starting out.

The only useful weapon in this dungeon is Pickaxe, so feel free to discard other weapons.<br/>The best time to identify items is after you've made the Behemoth Gate vanish on the floor.<br/>Always use <span class="blueText">Tap</span> type pots while standing on the stairs until Monster Pot is identified.<br/>Swing unidentified staves at a wall in a safe room until Vaulting Staff is identified,<br/>after which you can identify staves by swinging them at Behemoth Monsters.

### 11-20F

Aim for <span class="oceanBlueText">21+</span> Max HP since B-Pop Tank, B-Porky, and B-Dragon deal <span class="oceanBlueText">20</span> damage from afar.<br/>Use the <span class="blueText">Look Around</span> command often, as B-Crossbowboy can one-shot you with an arrow.<br/>Don't linger in rooms - B-Schubell summons <span class="oceanBlueText">1</span> Bashagga or Gigataur even from a distance.<br/>B-Bored Kappa and B-Hoppin' Batter are immune to projectiles without Far-throwing status.<br/>Starting from <span class="orangeText2">10F</span>, even number floors often have a room split by a waterway (ex: 10F, 12F...).

<h3 id="general-advice-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Red">General Advice</h3>

The basics <span class="purpleText2">Explore without being detected</span> and <span class="purpleText2">Pierce barriers to slay Behemoths</span> still apply.<br/>Far-throwing Bracelet greatly expands options for slaying Behemoths, so take good care of it.<br/>Remember to unequip Far-throwing Bracelet before throwing items if you spot a Bouncy Wall.<br/>The Behemoth monster table is far more manageable between <span class="orangeText2">1-59F</span> compared to <span class="orangeText2">60-99F</span>,<br/>so finish all preparations by the end of <span class="orangeText2">59F</span>, then switch to rushing stairs until the end.<br/>Behemoths like B-Frenzy Radish, B-Ultra Gazer, B-Soul Reaper, and B-Mini Tank spawn,<br/>so even a single quick mistake can cost you a Revival Grass or the run itself.

In addition to the above, it'd be good to keep the following 3 things in mind:

<h4 id="synthesize-shields-for-defensive-runes" class="heading4Red"><img src="../images/other/pot.png"/> Synthesize shields for defensive runes</h4>

- Synthesis Pot can randomly be obtained from making a Behemoth Gate vanish.
- Top priority = <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/29.png"/></span> Sating, <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/27.png"/></span> Guts, <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/18.png"/></span> Projectileproof, <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/10.png"/></span> Anti-Dragon
- High priority = <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/9.png"/></span> Keen, <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/19.png"/></span> Hypnosisproof, <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/20.png"/></span> Magic Cancel
- Medium & Low priority = <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/11.png"/></span> Anti-Blast (Medium), <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/2.png"/></span> Retribution (Low)

<h4 id="hunt-bashagga-or-gigataur-to-level-up" class="heading4Red"><img src="../images/monsters/14-4.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/11-4.png"/> Hunt Bashagga or Gigataur to level up</h4>

- These 2 monsters can spawn via a Monster House (Monstercall Scroll), Monster Pot, or shiny wall.
- Reaching <span class="oceanBlueText">Lv20+</span> hastens HP regen speed to <span class="oceanBlueText">+3</span> HP / Turn, making it easier to deal with fixed damage.
- Be careful not to get blown away by wind, maybe wait until you have a weapon to hunt these foes,<br/>and consider doing 2 sessions on different floors instead of cramming it into a single floor.
- Steps for Monster House Method:
    1. Prepare Monstercall Scroll (Blank Scroll) + Sanctuary Scroll → find a room with <span class="oceanBlueText">2</span> entrances.
    2. Place the Sanctuary Scroll near one of the entrances → read Monstercall Scroll.
    3. Equip a Pickaxe and hunt Bashaggas → Gigataurs until the 1st\~2nd wind gust occurs.
    4. Exit the room from the side where foes can't pursue → wait until foes leave the room.
    5. Enter the room and claim items → rush to the stairs.

<h4 id="warp-using-water-or-air-tiles" class="heading4Red"><img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> Warp using water or air tiles</h4>

- Use a Vaulting Staff to land on a water or air tile to instantly warp to a random room.
- You can also unequip a Waterwalk Bracelet while standing on a water tile to warp.
    - This can be done even during Sumo status, unlike the Vaulting Staff method.
- Tatsumi Shiren can use an ability to replicate the Vaulting Staff effect.

<h3 id="1-30f" class="heading3Red">1-30F</h3>

The same strategy that was used for the [first clear](#first-clear) can generally be used for this floor range.<br/>Exploring to make Behemoth Gates vanish for reward items is more important in the 2nd clear,<br/>so watch for Shield of Sating, and duplicate Fullness replenishing items with Bouncy Walls early on.<br/>Don't forget that Revival Grass (revive) and Fixer Scroll (read when starving) can replenish Fullness.<br/>Behemoths that appear on <span class="orangeText2">1-10F</span>, <span class="orangeText2">21-30F</span>, and <span class="orangeText2">51-59F</span> lean toward being close-ranged attackers,<br/>meaning those floor ranges are the easiest in terms of making Behemoth Gates vanish.

If you get an unidentified grass from a Behemoth Gate, consider saving it in case it's Revival Grass.<br/>Far-throwing and Waterwalk bracelets both significantly reduce the difficulty of this dungeon.<br/>Synthesis Pot is great for the 2nd clear, and can only be obtained from Behemoth Gates.<br/>The priority for identifying item categories should probably be: Bracelets &gt; Pots &gt; Grass.
